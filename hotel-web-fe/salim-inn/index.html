GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked questions.

Quick answers for a smoother arrival. Contact the 24-hour reception team if you need anything else.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in begins at 2:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon. Late checkout is subject to room availability and additional charges; a full-day rate may apply after 3:00 pm.

How is my booking confirmed?

Bookings are confirmed after full payment is received. Published payment methods include credit card, PayPal, and bank transfer.

What is the cancellation policy?

A refund may be available when cancellation notice is provided at least three days before arrival. With less notice—or for a no-show—the first night may be charged.

Does every room have Wi‑Fi and air-conditioning?

Yes. Free Wi‑Fi is available throughout Salim Inn, and guest rooms include air-conditioning and a television.

Where is Salim Inn located?

Salim Inn is at Lot 21–22, Lorong Salim 17 in Farley Commercial Centre, Sibu. Farley Supermarket is approximately a five-minute walk away.

Can I speak to someone at any time?

Yes. Reception operates 24 hours. Call +60 11-1050 7083 or email saliminnsibu@gmail.com.
